The Five Schools

A school is not a spell list. Everyone does the same two things with their hands, and any competent person can be taught the follow-through in a season. What cannot be handed over is library — and there are only so many ways a human being can come by one. A school is a doctrine of acquisition.

Why library is the whole argument

Recognition is not a read. Anything already in library resolves under a quarter-second at any acuity, because she is matching rather than measuring. So every school's fast tier is memory — which means how you get memory is the only thing worth splitting a trade over.

The five, and what kills each

The audit that mattered: do any two look the same on screen? If they do, one is redundant. Here they are side by side — judge the silhouettes, not the prose.

Matchers

Carry the library on your chest. 180 known chips, each identified by a count of nicks filed into one edge, read by thumb, in the dark, at a run.

Carried reagentsFlaskSet-led

Great at Turns unfamiliar material into FAST material better than anyone — matching is recognition, and recognition is under a quarter-second.

Dies to Anything that occupies one hand halves her. Cold and wet finish her outright: the nick-code needs bare fingertips.

Rods

Touch nothing. Read through an instrument you have already read through — and above all through seated metal in the forearm. Their line is nothing on the skin.

Pre-loaded chargesImplantSolve-led

Great at They own distance and deep extraction. Half the country’s ore is found by a man saying iron at forty feet in good faith.

Dies to State-blind, and it is not a minor axis. He reads half-set brass as superb brass — and half-set eats instruments, up sixty centimetres of rod, into the port at his wrist.

Surveyors

Walk the building days early and leave the lines in it. Then fight in a room where every surface is recognition and every boundary is already down.

The surveyed buildingPoolSet-led

Great at On owned ground, the most dangerous thing alive — and they own the runner, because everyone else’s is a prayer with a direction and hers is a rail.

Dies to Everything else. Off prepared ground she is the weakest thing in the game, and her library goes stale — damp, frost, a repaint, and the house is strange again.

Quicks

Read the living. Tissue is agony to read cold and trivial to recognise warm — every person is the same seven or eight families, so what varies is state, and state is all she ever measures.

Great at The best hands on any field: a break set in nine-tenths of a second and the man gets up. She is who they send when the reach found the gallery crew instead of ore.

Dies to She must be inside arm’s length, and every soldier alive has already decided not to let her touch them. In a siege she is baggage with good hands.

Take-off hands

Read from the record. This is a barracks of that vintage, so the door pins are wrought, three to a leaf. Recognition without ever having touched the thing.

Great at Disguise does not touch her — grease, blacking, dazzle-plate buy nothing against a woman who never looked at a surface in her life. The low-acuity grinder’s school.

Dies to She cannot check. A plausible lie about the vintage is nearly as good as beating her, and her errors always complete, because chained cannot be aborted.

The counter graph

Ten edges. Every school has exactly two predators and two prey — so nobody is unbeaten and nobody is a designated victim. Solid arrows are doctrine-killing; dashed are situational and survivable. The arrow points at the loser.

HARDSurveyors kill take-off hands. The counter-survey wing swaps three fittings between the record and the fight. She fires into a material that is not there — and she cannot check.
HARDTake-off hands kill quicks. The counter is the room, and a quick has no library for rooms. He only has to make the fight be about the bearing pins.
HARDQuicks kill rods. An instrument never returns state, and half-set is pure state. It eats his rod and travels up it into the port at his wrist. She also knows the four places a seat can go — her trade cuts them.
HARDRods kill matchers. She chains constantly at a quarter more sap and says her count out loud. He has none, concedes her opening burst, and keeps rod's length so she never gets a flat palm on the world.
HARDRods kill surveyors. Pool dies when the gate shuts upstream — and he does it from the cellar without entering the room.
SOFTMatchers over take-off hands. 180 chips of undated metal the record cannot describe — and she can file a fitting open in four seconds and prove the record wrong in front of the crew.
SOFTMatchers over surveyors. A foot of known cord slid under a door reads the far end as though her hand were there. Nobody scores a scullery.
SOFTSurveyors over quicks. Thirty feet of scored line never grants contact — though if a quick does get a palm on her it ends in nine-tenths of a second.
SOFTTake-off hands over rods. He packed his substance on a Tuesday; hers is the building. And his stance is a lean, so she takes the bearing out from under it.
SOFTQuicks over matchers. The case is read by nick-count under a bare fingertip. A set on the reading hand costs nine-tenths of a second.

One correction to my own document: it claims every school has one hard kill and one soft one. Not quite — matchers' two kills are both soft, rods' are both hard. The balance still holds at two-and-two for everyone, and I'd keep it: rods as the school with decisive matchups and matchers as the one that grinds out advantages is characterful. But the sentence is wrong and I'd rather you heard it from me.

Your call

Four decisions. Recommendations marked, but all four are yours.

Decision 1

Which schools are playable

Recommended — three, plus one bought

Take-off hands, quicks and matchers playable from the start. Rods playable but purchased: a mid-game doctrine you elect into by paying a seating fee and accepting a permanent hole in your body, with a real one-in-six failure. That is a progression event, not a character-creation checkbox.

Alternative — all four playable at start

Simpler, and it lets a player commit to rods early. Costs the single best piece of characterisation in the roster: that rods are the school where money and nerve stand in for what you were born with.

Decision 2

Surveyors enemy-only?

Recommended — enemy-only

Three days of prep then a fight you have already won is thrilling to lose to and dull to play, and a playable surveyor forces every mission into the same shape. As an enemy she gives you the fight that is a building — and teaches the best clever move in the setting: the best thing to find in a house is somebody else's chalk.

Alternative — playable in a prep-heavy mode

Real appeal for a certain player, and it makes the pool topology playable. But it needs its own mission structure to exist at all, which is a large bill for one school.

Decision 3

Where the protagonist starts

Recommended — take-off hand

A town, a chalk stub, and learning what a date stone means. Power arrives as knowledge: you walk into a room you have never entered and it is already yours. The arc is authorship — she asserts things about the world and the world pays for her being right, until the record is older than the people in the room and the drag on the floor is pointing back at her.

Alternative — quick

Hands on people. A break set in nine-tenths of a second and the man gets up. Power arrives as care. The arc is complicity — she learns a reach is aimable, that nobody can tell an aimed one from a dumb one afterward, and that the Law commands her to name a source and never says what a source may be.

Why take-off hand first: hour one teaches the access gate itself — recognition is earned by looking at a hinge, not granted by a stat — and the turn is delivered by the world rather than by her own choice, so she is capable and complicit before she has agreed to anything. Bring the quick in at mid-game, when the player has something to lose by learning what she learns.

Decision 4 — not on the gate list, and expensive

Two rulings this gate made for you

State comes free with recognition

On material already in library, state resolves at recognition speed at any acuity. Reverse it and both state-led schools become strong-acuity-only, the disreputable school becomes the most natally gated in the game, and acuity is not a power stat stops being true.

An instrument buys depth and nothing else

Never speed, never state. If a rod's instrument ever buys either, acuity becomes purchasable outright and the rod trade's whole class politics collapses into a shop.
