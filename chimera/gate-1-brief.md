# GATE 1 — THE BEDROCK

Foundry and mine were one idea in different overalls — a finite stock pulled from the ground —
so they are merged; the mine survives as grafts. The three below cannot all be true. Each answers
one question, *what is the charge*, and everything else falls out of the answer.

## OPTION A — THE FOUNDRY. Charge is heat.

Spending charge makes cold. Rich land is warm ground, carriers are cored stone held hot, implants
bank warmth under the skin. Every working writes its own receipt in a single frame — frost ring on
the flagstones, the caster's fingers going stupid, the field where the grass stopped four centuries ago. **It makes possible** legibility for free — a spectator with no
tooltip sees the cold and knows the price — map control a level designer can build, counterplay
available to any non-caster with a bucket, and a country whose entire apparatus is *the heating
of a cold place*: ordinary, useful, generational, monstrous underneath. **It costs** surprise. Heat is
the least strange answer here, so the whole burden of originality lands on SOLVE & SET; and cold is
a mild horror until worked hard, reading as discomfort right up to stage four.

- **Hard stroke:** SET. Solve takes one thing away; set must specify shape, extent, boundary, grain and body.
- **The Law:** *"Open nothing you can't close — and close it with your own hand."*
- **Charge:** HEAT. **Astral:** out — the sky is weather and a calendar.
- **Half-set:** slag. Grainless matter held open on the ground's warmth, creeping downhill, taking the grain of whatever touches it.
- **Reaching:** short of substance, the working runs the conduction path to the warmest, loosest thing present. Usually a person.
- **The dead:** a cold set. The corpse closes whole and grey, can never be opened again, and pulls warmth off the churchyard forever.
- **FAST:** iron pinched between two fingers, fist shut round it so the fist is the rim, solve inside the fist, punch the hand open along the throw — fingers set the width, the wrist sets the grain.

## OPTION B — THE DISPENSARY. Charge is a dose.

Charge only moves *through a body*. Land and carrier route through the caster, so the meter is a
person: tolerance, saturation, withdrawal, who gets the last clean ampoule. This is the most
literal reading of the locked apothecary register — medicine and atrocity share the kit, the hands
and the vocabulary. **It makes possible** the best Gate 3 in the set: a loophole that
cannot be done alone, because you route one heavy working through a line of people who each take
a dose that measurably harms none of them. Nobody dies, everybody agrees, a decent person signs
the order. **It costs** tonal control: tolerance is so mechanically loud it outweighs both
strokes and tips the thing into drug-magic, a register the brief invites and a genre it does not.
Worse, corpses are cleaner stock than the living, which redirects the horror from atrocity to
grave-robbing — a smaller idea.

- **Hard stroke:** SOLVE. The dose is metered, irreversible and interruptible; set is a release that always completes. Sets do not fail, they go wrong.
- **The Law:** *"Draw the line, measure the dose, and set what you solve."*
- **Charge:** DRAM. **Astral:** out — belief has never once changed a set.
- **Half-set:** matter loosened and never told what to be, taking its pattern from whatever touches it. Hazard, area denial and prepared-work technology at once.
- **Reaching:** the boundary fails and the working swells as a sphere until it finds what it lacks — thirty kilos of the nearest woman for two grams of iron.
- **The dead:** a cast. Complete, correct, warm for six hours, not decaying, because it is not dead — it is finished.
- **FAST:** palm flat to the wall so the hand is the boundary, thumb the ampoule at your cuff, one dram through the palm, sweep up and out — the wall comes with it as a ridge.

## OPTION C — THE CIRCUIT. Charge is a difference.

Not a stuff and not a quantity anyone owns: a maintained separation between two things. You store
separations, not power, and every working is a fall in progress hunting the shortest conducting
path — which in most rooms is a person. **It makes possible** the most original system here and the best
endgame by some distance: if power is a difference you hold, surrendering it means letting
yourself equalise with the world — a permanent physical act, not a gesture. Reaching becomes
indifferent physics instead of malice, and every witness sees who paid. **It costs** the locked register.
Pile, arc, earth, short and leak are not purity, dosage, residue and precipitate; this option
fights 2.5 and §6 for the whole project and drifts to steampunk whenever we stop steering. And 2.6
already assigns originality to SOLVE & SET and the topology, so C pays that cost for
distinctiveness we are getting elsewhere anyway.

- **Hard stroke:** SOLVE — you climb the gradient. Set is the fall: two tenths of a second, uninterruptible, only redirectable.
- **The Law:** *"It comes out of the room, so know the room."*
- **Charge:** TENSION. **Astral:** out, with one quarantined mystery — why the living hold a difference at all.
- **Half-set:** slack. Matter still at potential with no form to be, joining to whatever conducts to it, and the join is stronger than bone.
- **Reaching:** a thread arcs from the work to what it chose — contact over distance, wet over dry, living over dead.
- **The dead:** an earth. Correct in every particular, room-temperature forever, drawing endlessly, and nobody is in there.
- **FAST:** forearm broad to an iron banister, solve eighteen inches of rail, wrench the wrist outward — that turn is the set, and the rail comes off as a spike.

## RECOMMENDATION

**Take A** — it buys legibility, map control and non-caster counterplay from one physical fact,
its Gate 5 apparatus writes itself, and its weakness is a tuning problem where B's and C's are structural.

I differ from my own analysts on the route: they called C fatal because nothing lives inside a
person, which is wrong — 2.3 puts charge inside bodies by fiat, so C's antagonist is buildable.
C's real disqualifier is that it fights the locked register for the whole project.

## DECISIONS THIS GATE NEEDS FROM YOU

1. **Wording of the Law.** Every candidate's loophole broke on its own sentence. Pick the wording and the crack in it together, or Gate 3 has nothing to reveal.
2. **Name of the charge.** HEAT, DRAM or TENSION — it fixes whether charge can be stolen, stored, or given away.
3. **The astral layer.** All three say out. If you confirm it, the one permitted mystery must be an observed absence with no mechanism — the moment anything can read or move it, the dead come back.
4. **Vocabulary.** Every candidate ran two to three times over the ten-term cap by silently exempting the same classes: faction names, injury stages, units of measure. Rule whether those count.
5. **Not on your list — which stroke is hard.** §3.1 asks us to say; I am asking you, because it changes the feel in the hands more than the Law's wording does. Hard SET means everyone can open matter and the world is drowning in things nobody closed. Hard SOLVE means casters are precision surgeons and opening is the rare skill. Different games.
6. **Not on your list — is reaching aimable?** If a caster can lay a bad boundary beside your allies on purpose, decide it now. Now it is the villain's method; later it is a rule bolted on.

## GRAFTS AVAILABLE

- **Mine:** a ground-grade table doing map control, overdraw record and a century of national history in one object — plus *precision is the danger*: name a working exactly and it goes and fetches the shortfall.
- **Dispensary:** the chain-of-bodies loophole, the only candidate that structurally cannot be performed alone; and cleanup cheap enough that half-set matter left in a street is a moral read on everyone who walked past it.
- **Circuit:** the reach preference order as aimable targeting — contact, wet, warm, living — so a wet chain thrown across a carcass nominates a source.
